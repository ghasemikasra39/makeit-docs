FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is MakeIt? +
MakeIt is a desktop application that automates the gaming content creation pipeline. It processes your gameplay recordings, converts them to vertical format for social media, adds custom overlays, and automatically posts to TikTok and Instagram.
What platforms does MakeIt support? +
MakeIt currently supports posting to TikTok (via the Content Posting API) and Instagram (via the Graph API). Both platforms receive optimized vertical video content in 9:16 format.
Do I need a TikTok Developer account? +
Yes, to use the TikTok posting feature, you need a TikTok Developer account with access to the Content Posting API. This allows MakeIt to post videos directly to your TikTok account on your behalf.
Is my data safe with MakeIt? +
Yes. MakeIt is a local desktop application — all video processing happens on your machine. Your API credentials are stored locally and never sent to any third-party servers. Video files are only temporarily uploaded to AWS S3 (which you control) during the posting process and are automatically deleted afterward.
What video formats are supported? +
MakeIt supports most common video formats including MP4, MOV, MKV, and AVI. Videos are processed using FFmpeg, which provides broad format compatibility and high-quality encoding.
Can I customize the video overlays? +
Yes! MakeIt supports custom intros, outros, and text overlays. You can create branded content with your own visual elements to maintain a consistent look across all your posts.
What are the system requirements? +
MakeIt runs on macOS 12.0 or later. You'll need Python 3.11+ and FFmpeg installed. We recommend at least 8GB of RAM for smooth video processing, especially with high-resolution 4K content.
How does automatic posting work? +
After processing, videos are temporarily uploaded to your AWS S3 bucket to generate a public URL. This URL is then submitted to TikTok/Instagram's APIs for posting. Once the platform confirms the upload, the temporary file is automatically deleted from S3.
