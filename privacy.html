Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how MakeIt ("the Application", "we", "our") handles information when you use our desktop application for gaming content automation.

MakeIt is a personal automation tool designed for individual content creators. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • API Credentials: TikTok and Instagram API tokens that you configure in the application
  • AWS Credentials: Amazon S3 access keys for temporary file hosting
  • Video Content: Gameplay recordings that you process through the application

2.2 Information from Third-Party Platforms

When you connect your TikTok account, we receive:

  • OAuth access tokens (for posting on your behalf)
  • Your TikTok user ID
  • Post IDs for content we upload

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal information beyond what's needed for the API connections
  • We do not track your usage or behavior
  • We do not collect analytics or telemetry
  • We do not have access to your TikTok followers, messages, or other account data

3. How We Use Information

Data Type Purpose Storage Location
API Credentials Authenticate with TikTok/Instagram APIs to post content Local files on your computer only
Video Files Process and convert for social media posting Temporarily on AWS S3, deleted after posting
Post History Track successful posts and avoid duplicates Local SQLite database on your computer

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 Local Storage

All sensitive data (API keys, credentials, configuration) is stored locally on your computer in configuration files. We recommend:

  • Keeping your credentials file secure and not sharing it
  • Using your operating system's security features
  • Not committing credentials to version control

4.2 Temporary Cloud Storage

Video files are temporarily uploaded to Amazon S3 to provide URLs for TikTok's Content Posting API. These files are:

  • Uploaded to your own AWS S3 bucket (that you configure)
  • Automatically deleted after successful posting
  • Deleted within 24 hours even if posting fails

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only shared with:

  • TikTok: Video content and captions you choose to post
  • Instagram: Video content and captions you choose to post
  • Amazon Web Services: Temporary video file hosting (in your own bucket)

All sharing is initiated by you and necessary for the Application's core functionality.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

  • Access: All data is stored locally on your computer and you can view it anytime
  • Deletion: Delete any local files to remove your data completely
  • Revocation: Revoke TikTok/Instagram access through their respective settings pages
  • Disconnect: Remove your API credentials from the configuration to stop all API access

7. TikTok Data Usage

In compliance with TikTok's developer requirements:

  • We only request the minimum permissions needed (video.upload, video.publish)
  • We only access your account to post content you explicitly choose to share
  • We do not access your followers, following list, likes, or messages
  • We do not store TikTok data beyond what's needed for posting functionality
  • Access tokens are stored securely and only used for their intended purpose

8. Children's Privacy

MakeIt is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
